User Data Deletion

Last Updated: DEC 22, 2025

Introduction

Vastrayl respects your privacy and your right to control your personal data. This page explains how you can request deletion of your personal information collected through the Vastrayl mobile application.

How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your account and all associated data in the following ways:

Option 1: In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

You can permanently delete your account directly from the Vastrayl app:

  1. Open the Vastrayl app
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
  3. Confirm the deletion request

This will permanently remove your account, profile, photos, comments, ratings, and all associated data from our systems.

Option 2: Manual Deletion Request

If you cannot access the app, you can request data deletion by emailing us at:

contact.vastrayl@gmail.com

Please include your registered email address and the subject line: "Vastrayl Data Deletion Request".

Data Retention Period

Once a deletion request is confirmed, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, unless a longer retention period is required by law (e.g., for legal or regulatory purposes).

What Data Is Deleted

  • Account profile information (name, email, username, profile photo)
  • User-generated content (outfit photos, captions, ratings, comments)
  • Device and usage data linked to your account
  • Contest entries and participation records

What Data May Be Retained

We may retain certain data for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, as required by law. This data is restricted and not used for marketing or profiling.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Data Deletion Policy, please contact us at: contact.vastrayl@gmail.com
